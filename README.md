# 🚲 Cyclistic Bike-Share Analysis

## Objective
To analyze ride patterns between casual riders and annual members in order to understand user behavior differences and recommend strategies to convert casual users into members.

---

## Tools Used
- R (Data Cleaning & EDA)
- Excel (Quick checks & formatting)
- Tableau (Dashboard visualization)

---

##  Dataset
Public bike-share trip data (2023–2024)

---

## Data Cleaning Process
- Removed null and duplicate values
- Converted date/time columns
- Created new variables:
  - ride_length
  - day_of_week
  - user_type

---

## Analysis Performed
- Ride frequency by user type
- Peak usage days
- Average ride duration
- Monthly trends

---

##  Key Insights

- Casual riders take longer trips than members
- Members ride more consistently on weekdays
- Casual riders are more active on weekends
- Summer months show peak usage

---

##  Business Recommendations

- Introduce weekend membership discounts
- Launch targeted promotions for casual riders
- Improve marketing during peak summer months
- Offer trial membership campaigns

---

## 📊 Dashboard

<img width="613" height="510" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/54114911-5e71-4ac4-b8cb-66e838700b6f" />

**Members vs. casual riders** — Members dominate at 79% of all rides, but casual riders take significantly longer trips (16.3 min avg vs. 10.2 min for members), suggesting they're using bikes more for leisure.

**Commuter pattern** — The hourly chart makes this very clear: members spike sharply at 8am and 5pm, a classic commuter signature. Casual riders are spread more evenly through the afternoon.

**Weekend casual surge** — While members ride most on weekdays (Tuesday peak), casual ridership peaks on Saturday and Sunday — reinforcing the leisure vs. utility divide.

**Bike preference** — Classic bikes lead at 60%, electric at 38%, and docked bikes are marginal at only 1.3%.

**Monthly stability** — January and February volumes are nearly identical, with only a slight uptick in casual riders in February.
